<p>Until recently, choosing a Ghillie cap meant picking a colour. That's no longer true. The headwear range now runs three genuinely different constructions — trucker, dad cap and canvas — and they don't do the same job just because they all sit on your head. Here's what actually separates them, and which one earns its place depending on the day.</p>

<h2>What Actually Separates Them</h2>
<p>A trucker cap is built stiff on purpose. A structured mid-to-high crown, a canvas or foam front panel, and a mesh back that's there to vent heat rather than trap it — it holds its shape on a hook or in a bag, and the peak keeps its curve. Our <a href="https://ghillieuk.com/products/original-trucker-cap-lichen-green">Original Trucker Cap</a>, <a href="https://ghillieuk.com/products/torrish-trucker-cap">Torrish Trucker Cap</a> and <a href="https://ghillieuk.com/products/sutherland-trucker-cap">Sutherland Trucker Cap</a> all follow that brief, each with a slightly different crown depth and peak flex, but the same basic idea: structure and ventilation over anything else.</p>
<p>A dad cap is the opposite brief entirely. No foam, no mesh, just soft 100% cotton in a low, unstructured crown that collapses flat when you take it off. The <a href="https://ghillieuk.com/products/tarvie-cap-ivy-green">Tarvie Cap</a> is built exactly to that spec — a washed finish, a two-tone peak, and a brass rear fastener rather than a snap. It sits lower and closer to the head than any trucker cap in the range, and it looks more lived-in the more you wear it rather than less.</p>
<p>A canvas cap sits between the two. The <a href="https://ghillieuk.com/products/rollock-canvas-cap-tan">Rollock Canvas Cap</a> keeps a trucker cap's baseball silhouette and brass buckle adjuster, but swaps the mesh back and foam front for a single durable canvas build with a medium-to-shallow crown — it sits close rather than tall, with none of the ventilation panel but also none of the collapse-flat softness of a dad cap. Structurally, it's the middle ground.</p>

<h2>The Weather and Activity Call</h2>
<p>Heat management is the real dividing line. On a fast dog walk or anything that gets you moving, the trucker cap's mesh back is doing genuine work — it's the only one of the three built to vent heat rather than hold it in. On a slower morning, standing at a gate or wandering round a farm shop, that ventilation stops being an advantage and just becomes wind getting through the back panel; the dad cap or canvas cap sit warmer and don't have that gap.</p>
<p>Low sun is where the trucker cap's stiffer peak earns its keep too — it holds a fixed curve and shades properly, where a soft dad cap peak can fold or lift in wind. If you're layering a hood over the top, the dad cap and canvas cap both sit flatter and lower than a trucker cap's higher crown, so they don't fight the hood's shape the way a structured front panel can.</p>
